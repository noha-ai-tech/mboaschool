## Shared visual direction

- Human-scale urban campus in Douala.
- Low ivory buildings with deep navy structural accents and restrained warm ochre trim.
- Blue metal roofs, breeze-block ventilation, covered walkways, paved courtyard and tropical planting.
- Warm, natural late-afternoon documentary photography.
- No phone number, slogan, school level, official seal, accreditation claim or personal data.
- The only permitted facade wording is `GUYSKULL`.
- Synthetic people are secondary, generic and non-identifying.
